# job-matching
A general job market clearinghouse, with the purpose of reducing both side friction and increasing long term match scores.


# The Current Issue 
The job market is seemingly locking into a new Cold War. Both sides, labor and employers are rapidly arming to defend and advance their respective interests. This new Cold War is leading to both decreased and increased frictions across the application market. 

# The Fix
The fix has been quietly working in the background for years in the medical residency process. The Gale-Shapley clearinghouse matches the best medical students with their best medical program given the market and each sides position in it.
